Engagement model

Bring me a business problem, not a perfectly written specification.

I can help turn an unclear requirement into an implementation plan, identify the riskiest technical assumptions, build the feature and document the trade-offs.

01

Understand

Clarify the user flow, constraints, existing system and the outcome that matters.

02

De-risk

Prototype or inspect the risky parts first: APIs, background behavior, scale, AI quality or third-party dependencies.

03

Build

Implement in the existing stack where possible, with maintainable interfaces and practical observability.

04

Ship

Validate production behavior, edge cases and handoff details instead of stopping at a happy-path demo.
