How the lessons work

Less “definition dump”. More engineering thinking.

The course is being shaped around a repeatable learning loop: understand the concept, see it in a realistic scenario, inspect code or architecture, then learn the trade-off.

1

Concept

What the thing is and why it exists.

2

Example

A concrete product or backend scenario.

3

Build

Code, config, API or architecture sketch.

4

Trade-off

Where it breaks and what you choose instead.
