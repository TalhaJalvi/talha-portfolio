Solomon AI - Music Recognition App

If you would like to delete your Solomon AI account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to info@maetragroup.com with the following details:

  • Subject: Delete My Solomon AI Account
  • Body: Include the email address associated with your Solomon AI account

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon processing your request, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your account credentials and profile information
  • Your music recognition history and saved results
  • Your Solomon AI chat history
  • Any preferences and settings associated with your account

Processing Time

We will process your deletion request within 30 days of receiving your email. You will receive a confirmation once your account and data have been deleted.

Contact

If you have any questions about the deletion process, please contact us at:

Email: info@maetragroup.com
