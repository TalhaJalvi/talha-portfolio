Product direction

Not another passive system design course.

Written guides explain concepts. Labs let you experience the consequences. The roadmap is intentionally built around experimentation rather than memorizing a reference diagram.

How simulation works

Modeled systems, not fake certainty.

Real server capacity depends on hardware, code, queries and workload. Each lab therefore publishes its assumptions explicitly and evaluates your architecture inside that scenario. The goal is learning the reasoning process: demand → capacity → bottleneck → trade-off.
